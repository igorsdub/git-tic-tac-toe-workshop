# Facilitator Training Notes

## User Learning Profile & Preferences
- **Focus**: Track A - Facilitator Preparation for delivering the 2-hour Git Tic-Tac-Toe Workshop.
- **Audience**: Novices, researchers, and students.
- **Priority**: Flawless live demo execution, timeboxing, and rapid error triage during pair play.

## Working Notes
- Workshop requires zero programming runtime dependencies for participants.
- Core constraint to defend: Guarantee at least 60 uninterrupted minutes of pair play (00:40 to 01:40).
- Key pedagogy: Never stage synthetic errors; authentic mishaps are the prime teaching moments.
