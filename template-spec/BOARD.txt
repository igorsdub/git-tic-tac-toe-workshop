# Canonical Board Format

The template's root `BOARD.txt` contains exactly three rows of three cells. A single space separates the cells; `.` is empty, and uppercase `X` and `O` are the player marks.

```text
. . .
. . .
. . .
```

One legal move replaces one `.` with the current player's mark. No divider, bracket, fence, metadata, or instruction line belongs in the canonical file. The validator and Game Wall also continue to read historical pipe-separated and bracketed boards; they normalize both formats to the same 3×3 grid.
