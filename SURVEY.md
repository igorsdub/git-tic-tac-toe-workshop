1. **How do you keep track of changes to research files?**

2. **How do you usually work on files with other people?**

3. **How have you used an AI coding assistant?**

   - I have accepted code suggestions while editing in an editor.
   - I have asked an agent to edit files for me.

4. **Which of these sound like your regular work?**
